REWORLD

Earth needs a new home and you're in charge of building it. We're playing Reworld, a space game where you grab tech, pack your ship, then land and build a planet. It's easy to learn if you spend 5 minutes on this page first.

The one rule to remember:
last in, first out.

In the first half you stack tiles on your ship. In the second half you can only take them off from the end, newest first. Load in the order you'll want to unload.

First-game tips

  • Plan the unload while you load.
  • Put a robot ahead of the builders that need it.
  • Grab robots early, while they're worth 4.
  • Don't leave a scoring contest empty. Each one costs you 3.
